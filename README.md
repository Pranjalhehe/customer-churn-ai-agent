# 🤖 Customer Churn AI Agent

AI-powered customer churn prediction and retention agent using **SVM** and **Neural Network** models.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Customer Success teams manage hundreds of customer accounts and may only discover that a customer is unhappy after they have already decided to cancel or stop using a product.

Our system aims to identify customers who are **at risk of churning before they leave**, understand the warning signals behind the prediction, and recommend personalized retention actions.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Predict whether a customer is likely to churn.
* Compare **SVM** and **Neural Network** models.
* Identify important factors associated with churn.
* Classify customers based on their churn risk.
* Explain why a customer may be at risk.
* Recommend personalized retention actions.

## 🧠 Models

### Support Vector Machine (SVM)

Used as a classification model to predict whether a customer is likely to churn.

### Neural Network

Used to learn more complex patterns in customer behavior and predict churn.

## 🔄 Basic Workflow

```text
Customer Data
      ↓
Data Preprocessing
      ↓
SVM + Neural Network
      ↓
Churn Prediction
      ↓
Risk Analysis
      ↓
AI Agent
      ↓
Reason + Recommended Action
```
